# Support and Feedback Policy

[日本語](SUPPORT.ja.md)

This repository is in a maintainer-led beta. General support requests,
unsolicited Issues, and unsolicited pull requests are not accepted during
this period.

## Request prior agreement

Before opening an Issue or pull request, or sending beta feedback, contact the
maintainer by direct message on X:

- [@akkiee76](https://x.com/akkiee76)

Briefly describe what you want to report or propose and wait for confirmation
before submitting it to the repository. Do not send private source code,
credentials, personal data, or undisclosed vulnerability details in the direct
message. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities.

The maintainer may announce a limited feedback channel for a specific beta or
evaluation round. When such a channel is open, useful reports include:

- The agent, product version, model, and skill revision used.
- A minimal change or public reproduction repository.
- The review request and the relevant repository instructions.
- The expected finding and the actual result.
- Why the result is a reproducible defect or a clear accuracy improvement.

Do not submit private source code, credentials, personal data, book proofs, or
other material you do not have permission to disclose.

The maintainer decides whether a report fits the project goals, reproduces it
against the evaluation suite, and makes any resulting change. Sending a report
does not create a commitment to provide individual support or accept a patch.

For a suspected vulnerability in this repository or its distribution process,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of discussing it publicly.
