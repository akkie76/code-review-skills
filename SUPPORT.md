# Support and Feedback Policy

[日本語](SUPPORT.ja.md)

This repository is in a maintainer-led beta. Evidence-backed Issues are welcome
when they help reproduce a defect or evaluate and improve the Skill. Responses,
fixes, and timelines are not guaranteed. Pull requests require prior agreement.

## Issues we accept

Open an Issue using the closest repository template for:

- A reproducible defect in the Skill, packaging, or installation process.
- A false positive, missed finding, or severity mismatch produced during an
  evidence-backed evaluation.
- A Codex or Claude Code compatibility problem.
- A proposal with a concrete example showing a clear accuracy improvement.
- Incorrect or unclear documentation.

Use the template fields and provide the smallest public reproduction or
sanitized evidence that demonstrates the behavior. Useful reports include:

- The agent, product version, model, and skill revision used.
- A minimal change or public reproduction repository.
- The review request and the relevant repository instructions.
- The expected finding and the actual result.
- Why the result is a reproducible defect or a clear accuracy improvement.

## Out of scope

The Issue tracker is not a channel for individual code-review requests,
project-specific consulting, general code-review questions, model-provider
support, or feature requests without a reproducible example. The maintainer may
close reports that cannot be reproduced or do not fit the project goals.

Do not submit private source code, credentials, personal data, book proofs, or
other material you do not have permission to disclose.

## Pull requests and other contact

Before opening a pull request, contact the maintainer by direct message on X
and obtain agreement on the scope:

- [@akkiee76](https://x.com/akkiee76)

Do not send private source code, credentials, personal data, or undisclosed
vulnerability details in the direct message. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
for suspected vulnerabilities.

The maintainer decides whether a report fits the project goals, reproduces it
against the evaluation suite, and makes or accepts any resulting change.
Opening an Issue does not create a commitment to provide individual support,
accept a patch, or respond by a particular date.

For a suspected vulnerability in this repository or its distribution process,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of discussing it publicly.
