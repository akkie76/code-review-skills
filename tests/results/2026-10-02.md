not an independent regrading of raw transcripts. The review texts, stream

matches and invocation observations below are reported results.

- Grader: Codex CLI `0.160.0`, model `gpt-5.5`, evaluating the review against

  fixed expectations. It is separate from Skill invocation and full
  output-contract compliance.

## Limits and follow-up

- Each pair was run once; no repeated-run reliability or invocation rate can
  be inferred. Positive cases were not run in Japanese in this sample.
- A different model family graded the output, but this record does not include
  raw traces or grader JSON and cannot independently confirm its decisions.
- Project-only setting sources do not prove user-level Skills or memory were
  excluded from the Claude Code environment.
- The two negative runs tested the host's unassisted output, not the installed
  Skill's suppression of false positives. That activation problem remains
  open under #34 rather than being hidden inside the aggregate score.
- The clarification that `may_report` can match a substantiated point inside
  a required finding applies to later evaluations. This sample was not
  retroactively regraded.

This record completes #26's evaluation-and-documentation work; it does not
resolve #34 or #35 or establish a general accuracy rate.
